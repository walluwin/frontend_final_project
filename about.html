About ArtNova

Art that lives on your screen

We connect independent digital artists with people who want something more personal than a stock wallpaper.

Our story

Started by three students who love design

ArtNova began as a university project: we noticed that young artists in Kazakhstan had no simple place to sell digital work, and buyers had no easy way to find original art instead of copied images.

Every artwork in our store is original, generated or drawn by its author, and sold with a clear license.

  • 100% original artworks, no stock images
  • Artists keep 80% of every sale
  • Clear personal and commercial licenses

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What file formats do I get?

Every purchase includes a vector SVG file and PNG exports in 4K and 8K resolution.

What is the difference between licenses?

Personal use covers your devices and prints for your home. Commercial use lets you use the artwork in a business, for example on a menu, website or product.

Can I sell my own art on ArtNova?

Yes! Write to us through the contact page and choose "I am an artist" as the topic.

Do you have discounts?

Subscribe to our newsletter on the home page to get a 10% promo code (hint: try ART10 in the cart).
